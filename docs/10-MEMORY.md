- **D-023** · 2026-09-10 · **The hero's Drip change is a wheel, not a roulette, a carousel, a slider or a fade — and each Drip is one flat transparent still, not a 36-frame turntable.** Mateo wrote the correction himself: "olvidate de ruleta, de carrusel horizontal, de slider y de fade. No es nada de eso." It replaces the roulette of D-021 and retires — for now, not permanently — the interactive turntable of D-022.

The construction is literal, because the metaphor is the specification. A wheel of radius **1.6 viewport widths** has its centre far below the hero; the six Drips hang on its rim, spaced **26°** apart; only the top of the arc is on screen. One step rotates the wheel **counter-clockwise** by that angle, and every Drip **counter-rotates** the same angle on its own axis so it stays vertical while it orbits — without that it is a carousel horse, which is exactly what was rejected. The incoming Drip rises from the lower right along the arc and settles centred; the outgoing crests over the apex and falls away to the left. Both move on the same path at the same speed, because it is one movement. Duration **1.05 s**, `power2.inOut`, no bounce.

Three numbers were chosen by measuring, not by taste, and should not be "tidied":
- **26° of separation.** The step angle is decided by the hole. Measured at 1440: at 42° the outgoing has gone and the incoming has not arrived for **40 % of the transition** and the hero is empty; at 26° that falls to **4 %**, so a Drip is always on screen and the movement reads as continuous.
- **1.6 viewport widths of radius.** The bigger the radius, the flatter the arc. At 3 widths the Drip rises 22 px across the whole visible span of a phone and reads as a straight slide; at 1.6 it rises 66 px on a phone and 92 px on desktop over the same span, and the curve is legible. It is still far larger than the viewport, which is the part that matters.
- **The fade is tied to the column width, never to the angle.** Inside its own column a Drip is always at 100 % opacity — that is what keeps the change from reading as a crossfade. It only dims once it has left the column, and on a phone the column is the whole screen, so there is no visible fade at all there. On desktop the outgoing crosses **behind** the headline at roughly half opacity, so it does not eat the H1's contrast.

Assets: six flat, transparent, front-facing stills — `web/src/content/drips.ts` builds every path in **one** function, `suero(slug, ext)`, so the day the six background-free SVGs exist the swap is that one function and nothing else. Until they exist the stills are frame 0 of the D-022 renders, which are the only transparent, realistic images of all six that exist.

**Six Drips, not eight.** 07 and 08 were proposed by this project and have no name, colour or image in any source; they leave the hero until the clinic defines them (they are the subject of an existing client question, not a new decision).

`prefers-reduced-motion`: no arc, direct change. Reopen if: the six SVGs arrive with a different aspect ratio (one constant, `RELACION`), or Mateo asks for autoplay, which is still off because `docs/05` §10 forbids it for the carousel.

- **D-024** · 2026-09-10 · **The hero rotates on its own, and the six bags are the brand's own artwork.** Two things Mateo decided in one line ("borralos y dale autoplay") plus the folder he handed over.

**Autoplay.** Four seconds of stillness per Drip, then the wheel steps. `docs/05` §10 forbids autoplay for the Drip **carousel** and that stands; this is the hero, and it is amended there. Five brakes, any one of which stops it: the pause button, mouse or focus inside the hero, the section scrolled out of view, the tab in the background, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. The pause button is not decoration — WCAG 2.2.2 requires a mechanism to stop anything that moves automatically for more than five seconds, so without it the hero fails. Two more details that are easy to get wrong: hover only pauses for `pointerType === "mouse"`, or the first tap on a phone would pause it forever; and the live region goes silent while it rotates, because otherwise a screen reader announces a new Drip every four seconds (WAI-ARIA APG, carousel). A step is a `setTimeout` re-armed on every change, not an interval, so pressing an arrow gives back the full four seconds instead of landing on the tail of the one already running. The Drip's name fades in **at 45 % of the step**, so it arrives with its bag instead of switching in frame zero while the previous bag is still on screen.

**The bags.** Mateo delivered six files as SVG. They are not vectors: each one is two base64 PNGs of 1070 × 1470 inside an SVG wrapper — one the picture, one a luminance mask carrying the alpha — at 945–1037 KB each, 5.9 MB for the six, and gzip barely touches base64. They were decoded, recomposed as RGBA, trimmed, placed on **one common canvas** of 692 × 1476 (per-bag crops make the bag change size as the wheel turns) and re-encoded: **35–39 KB each in AVIF, 218 KB for all six**. Same picture, 27× lighter. Kept in `web/public/img/drips/SOURCES.md`; the SVGs stay as the master.

The 288 turntable frames of D-022 were deleted with them — 296 files, 7.0 MB. `tools/drips/` still regenerates them if a turntable is ever wanted again.

Two things to know about the artwork: the liquid colours in `drips.ts` were **not** changed to match, because those come from the identity manual's own renders and are the light values that work as text on verde — the sampled ones are the same hues in shadow. And the labels carry a `PACIENTE:` field filled with invented personal names; illegible at hero size, but if the bags are ever shown large, ask for a version with that field blank.

- **D-025** · 2026-09-11 · **The hero wheel tilts, the palette comes from the label, and nothing moves on its own.** Mateo marked up a screenshot and drew the transition by hand. Supersedes the counter-rotation of D-023, the autoplay of D-024 and the invented dark grounds that were never recorded.

**The transition.** The bags are fixed to the rim of the wheel like spokes and **tilt with it** — no counter-rotation. Each step turns the wheel 90° counter-clockwise: the incoming bag starts lying down at the bottom right and straightens as it climbs to the apex; the outgoing one leans left as it goes down and ends lying down at the bottom left. 90° and not 60°, because at 60° the resting neighbour sat at mid-height, nearly upright and in view. The radius is a CSS variable, **0.8 × bag height**. A first pass used `max(42vw, 1.15 × bag height)` so the neighbours would fall outside the section at rest — and at mid-step both bags were outside too: on a phone the hero stood empty for half a second. At 0.8 the crossing happens on screen, outgoing going down the left while incoming shows on the right, as in the drawing. The resting neighbours are hidden by opacity instead: 100 % up to 50°, zero at 85°, which is the fade Mateo allowed from the start for bags moving away from the apex; at the 45° crossing both are fully opaque. 1.2 s, `power2.inOut`. D-023 asked for counter-rotation in writing as "CRÍTICO"; the drawing of 2026-09-11 is the later instruction and wins.

**No autoplay.** "No quiero que pase solo": the wheel only turns on an arrow click or the keyboard. The pause button, the five brakes and the silenced live region of D-024 went with it — without movement there is nothing to pause, which also closes the WCAG 2.2.2 question. The arrow keys are ignored while focus is in a form field.

**The palette is the label's.** Header and hero share six CSS variables on `<html>` (`--p-fondo`, `--p-texto`, `--p-acento`, `--p-halo`, `--p-cta-fondo`, `--p-cta-texto`), tweened for exactly the length of a step so the colour travels with the bag. Source of truth: `PALETAS` in `web/src/content/drips.ts`; resting values (Drip 01, verde) in `globals.css`, so the page is right without JavaScript and every other page gets verde. ## Gotchas (inherited system, found 2026-09-08/09)

- **G-001** · `firestore.rules`: every collection readable and writable by any authenticated user; `usuarios` included → self-promotion to admin. Authorization lives only in the UI.
- **G-002** · Deactivating a user (`activo:false`) is checked only in `login.html`; the Firebase Auth account keeps full data access via the SDK.
- **G-003** · Client-side sign-up may be enabled in Firebase Auth; if so, anyone with the public config passes `isAuth()`. Unverified — needs console access (CI-05).
- **G-004** · Permission mismatches: `sueros.html` accepts `stock|formulas`, sidebar grants `sueros`; dashboard CRM card needs `leads`, which admin cannot grant; `consentimiento.html` checks no permission. Resolved by §0.5 of the rules doc (client decides) and a new `crm` flag.
- **G-005** · `manual.html` shows `allow read, write: if true` as the deployed rules and says stock is decremented at cash; the code decrements at nursing start. Documentation that is wrong.
- **G-006** · `config/recordatorios`, `config/archivado`, `config/disp_{profesionalId}` exist in admin; nothing implements them. Dead configuration (rules §0.7).
- **G-007** · Turno states spelled differently per screen (`ESPERA`/`EN RECEPCIÓN`, `TRATAMIENTO`/`EN TRATAMIENTO`, lowercase sets on the dashboard). A turno moved from the agenda can be invisible in nursing.
- **G-008** · The repository snapshot is not the deployed code: `index (4).html` is a browser download duplicate of the `index.html` every link targets; `jspdf.umd.min.js` is 0 bytes; Netlify receives a zip by drag-and-drop. - **G-010** · All items of one ticket share the same `OI` number by design; `nro_comp` is unique only for EGRESO/CIERRE rows. Do not add a blanket unique index.

## Gotchas (the wheel, found 2026-09-10, WU-04)

- **G-045** · A grid item with `z-index: auto` does **not** create a stacking context, so the per-Drip `z-index` the wheel writes to order its own children escaped to the section level and beat the copy's `z-10` — the outgoing bag passed **in front of** the H1. - **G-046** · An absolutely positioned box with `width: auto` around an `<img>` collapses to **0 px** under Tailwind's reset, because `img { max-width: 100% }` resolves against the shrink-to-fit parent whose width depends on the image — a circular constraint. The bag was in the DOM, correctly transformed, and invisible. Always give the box a definite `aspect-ratio`; it also fixes the layout before the image lands, so CLS stays at 0.
- **G-064** - 2026-09-12 - Python's io.open in text mode on Windows turns every newline into CRLF, and with core.autocrlf=true git did not normalize it back on `git add`: `docs/09-WORKLOG.md` became the only CRLF file in the repo and its next commit showed all 287 lines rewritten instead of the six that changed. - **G-063** · `pnpm typecheck` green says nothing about `pnpm lint`, and CI runs both on every push. A whole day of WU-04 was gated on typecheck alone; the first lint before committing found two real errors that had been sitting in the code for hours: `setState` called directly inside a `useEffect` in `Hero.tsx` (`react-hooks/set-state-in-effect`, fixed with `useSyncExternalStore` for `prefers-reduced-motion`) and the header logo navigating with `<a href="/">` instead of `next/link` (`@next/next/no-html-link-for-pages`). Run lint together with typecheck after each block, not only before the commit.
- **G-062** · A panel with `overflow-y: auto` that Flip grows from a small box shows a classic grey Windows scrollbar while it grows (and whenever it overflows). The services detail panel hides the bar (`scrollbar-width: none` and `::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }`) and signals overflow with a faded bottom edge (a mask) that lands on the padding when everything fits.
- **G-061** · A line hidden by moving it `yPercent: 105` inside an `overflow: hidden` mask is not hidden when the text uses `type-display` (`text-box: trim-both` trims the line box to cap height and baseline): ascenders and accents stick out of the trimmed box and stay inside the mask. - **G-060** · With the Browser pane hidden (`document.visibilityState` "hidden", G-048) not only rAF stalls: scroll events and every ScrollTrigger and GSAP update stall too, so what is measured there is wrong (the header never compacted, colours never changed, the intro sat half-way). Fallback used on 2026-09-11: headless Chromium through the Playwright bundled with the global `@playwright/cli` (`require` of `<npm global>/@playwright/cli/node_modules/playwright`, `executablePath` pointed at the installed `ms-playwright/chromium_headless_shell-1234`, because the bundled version expects 1243 and downloading was not needed), scripted from the scratchpad; the screenshots are real files that can be sent to Mateo.
- **G-059** · A ScrollTrigger per section with `onToggle` cannot drive a state that must also hold at the very top of the page or after a jump. With the header in normal flow, at scroll 0 no section has reached the header yet, and `window.scrollTo(0)` from the services section jumped over the hero's active range without ever making it active, so the header stayed white over the grey hero. Pick the state in one page-wide trigger (`start: 0, end: "max"`) from the section borders' `start` values, and apply it in `onRefresh` as well.
- **G-058** · `focus()` on an element that a Tailwind data-attribute variant hides (`group-data-[abierta=true]:hidden`) does nothing while the attribute still hides it. Closing a services card focused "Abrir" right after starting the close animation and focus fell to `<body>`; it has to happen after `data-abierta` flips, in the same callback.
- **G-057** · Plain CSS written outside a cascade layer beats every Tailwind 4 utility, because Tailwind puts utilities in `@layer utilities` and unlayered styles win over any layer. `.link-linea { position: relative; display: inline-flex }` in globals.css silently cancelled `absolute` and `hidden` on the same element: the desktop "Ver más" sat in the card's flow and the phone showed it although it was `hidden`, which added about 107 px to every card and left the animated backgrounds at 0 px. Custom component classes go inside `@layer components`.
- **G-056** · A WAAPI `clip-path: circle()` animation looked smooth on desktop and froze on a phone: clip-path is not reliably composited, so it stalls whenever the main thread is busy. For a growing reveal, scale a solid disc (`transform`) inside an `overflow: hidden` container; `transform` and `opacity` are the only properties the compositor is guaranteed to run by itself (motion-web-senior/performance).
- **G-055** · Never let React render a value that a GSAP tween also owns. A component that renders `style={{ transform, opacity }}` from props and ALSO animates those properties imperatively gets its values reset by React on every re-render; any write-skipping cache on the animation side then leaves the element wrong until the cache happens to change. Render the initial state once (freeze it with `useState(initial)`) and hand the property to the animation after mount.
- **G-054** · Editing the children of a SplitText headline (the hero H1) while the page is open makes Fast Refresh throw React "removeChild: the node to be removed is not a child of this node": SplitText moved those nodes into its line wrappers. Next then does a full reload and the page is fine. Dev-only; confirmed with a fresh load and two wheel steps without errors (2026-09-11). Not a bug to fix, a reason to reload after touching the H1.
- **G-053** · A `<script>` in the root layout `<head>` that adds a class to `<html>` before hydration works, but costs two console errors in dev: a hydration mismatch on `<html>` and React's "Encountered a script tag while rendering React component", and in production it needs a CSP hash. Gate first-frame visibility with CSS `:has()` instead and let the component add the class.
- **G-052** · In the Browser pane's viewport emulation, `svh`/`vh` resolve against the real pane window, not the emulated size: at an emulated 375 x 812, `40svh` measured 468 px, and at an emulated 1440 x 900 the hero filled half the frame. Judge height-based layout in a real browser window, not in the emulation.
- **G-051** · With `writing-mode: vertical-rl`, `display: flex` stacks the children along the inline axis, which is vertical: two words end up glued in the SAME column ("EscudoAntioxidante"). Two plain block children stack as columns, right to left, which is what a two-line vertical name needs.
- **G-050** · `scrollWidth` does not reveal a headline that overflows inside a SplitText line mask: the mask clips (`overflow: clip`), so `scrollWidth` returns the box width and reports no overflow while the last letters are cut. Measure with a `Range` (`range.selectNodeContents(h1).getBoundingClientRect()`): its box is the union of the line wrappers and the text, so it equals the column width when the text fits and exceeds it when the text overflows (435 against 430 at 1024 was the tell). - **G-049** · In a grid container, `order` changes **painting** order, not only placement. The hero's bag column carries `order-first` so it sits above the copy on phones; with no explicit z-index that also made the grid paint it before an absolutely positioned circle declared earlier in the DOM, and the circle covered the bag. The DOM said the image was on screen, loaded and fully opaque — only `elementFromPoint` and a screenshot showed it hidden. Give every layer of a composed hero an explicit z-index.

Neither was real; a forced paint showed the correct frame. `document.hidden` stays false in that state, so the component's own visibility gate does not catch it. Do not measure motion by sampling styles over time in a hidden pane. - **G-047** · Screenshots taken back-to-back in one `browser_batch` can be captured **before** the paint that a preceding `javascript_tool` call triggered — two frames came back empty and looked like a bug in the animation.
